OHCOW Heat Stress (Health Standard)

Occupational Health Standard
Adjusted Index
--
SAFE
Formula Technical Specification (OHCOW):
Humidex = T + 0.5555 × (e - 10.0)
Parameters:
• T: Air Temperature (°C)
• e: Vapor Pressure (hPa), derived from Temperature and Relative Humidity (RH).
• Adjusted Index: Humidex + Clothing Adj. + Radiant Adj.

OSHA (WBGT Assessment)

Measure or estimate the Wet Bulb Globe Temperature in the work area.

Used for personalized hydration and sweat rate estimation.

Heat Index
--
SAFE
Formula Technical Specification (OSHA/ACGIH):
M_adj = M_base × (W / 70)
Result = Is WBGT_eff < ACGIH_TLV(M_adj)?
Parameters:
• W: Worker Body Weight (kg)
• M_base: Standard Metabolic Rate (Watts) based on workload classification.
• M_adj: Adjusted Metabolic Rate accounting for body mass.
• WBGT_eff: Effective WBGT (Measured + Clothing Adjustment).
• ACGIH TLV: Threshold Limit Value curve for safe heat exposure.

WBGT Monitoring

WBGT (Wet Bulb Globe Temperature) is the gold standard for heat stress monitoring.

Outdoor (Sun) = 0.7T_nwb + 0.2T_g + 0.1T_db

NOAA Heat Index
Apparent Temperature
--
SAFE
Formula Technical Specification (NOAA HI):
HI = -42.38 + 2.05T + 10.14R - 0.22TR - 0.007T² - 0.05R² + 0.001T²R + 0.0008TR² - 0.000002T²R²
Parameters:
• T: Air Temperature (°F)
• R: Relative Humidity (%)
• HI: Heat Index (°F) - Apparent Temperature

Actual Vapor Pressure (e)
--
Formula Technical Specification:
e = e_s(T_w) - A · P · (T_d - T_w)
RH = 100 · (e / e_s(T_d))
Parameters & Constants:
• T_d, T_w: Dry & Wet Bulb temperatures (°C).
• e_s(T): Saturation vapor pressure at temp T (Tetens Equation).
• A: Psychrometer constant (~0.00066 for standard sling psychrometers).
• P: Atmospheric pressure (hPa).

Core Concept

Human Heat Balance

Heat Stress occurs when total heat load exceed the body's capacity to dissipate heat via evaporation and convection.

Safety Tip

Clothing Factor

Impermeable clothing (like PPE) prevents sweat evaporation, increasing risk even at lower ambient temperatures.

Proactive Care

Hydration Strategy

Do not wait for thirst. Drink small amounts of water frequently (every 15-20 mins) in high-risk zones.
